When the user types `???` they are asking for Claude's thoughts, suggestions, opinions, or recommendations — not a literal question. Treat it as an invitation to weigh in proactively.

**Why:** User convention to avoid typing a full question; shorthand for "what do you think about this?"

**How to apply:** Whenever `???` appears in a message, respond with relevant thoughts, tradeoffs, recommendations, or concerns about the topic at hand.
